CASE / 01 OPERATING MODEL

Give experimentation
a healthier rhythm.

I combined workflow data with team feedback to find where experiments waited, where ownership blurred, and what would make delivery easier to predict.

Role
Business Operations Manager
Focus
Experiment lifecycle · cross-team flow
Work
Audit · operating model · reporting

The challenge

Experiment work crossed product, analytics, engineering, and design. Long queues and inconsistent handoffs made it hard to see what was active, blocked, or ready for a decision.

What I did

  • Mapped the lifecycle from hypothesis through analysis and rollout.
  • Compared lead time, cycle time, active work, and team feedback.
  • Clarified work types, ownership, readiness checks, and service expectations.

Outcome

Designed a cross-functional A/B testing operating model that reduced experiment cycle time by 20% and accelerated hypothesis validation.

CASE / 02 DECISION FRAMEWORK

Make priority
visible and explainable.

I designed a shared scoring approach so teams could discuss customer value, urgency, effort, and strategic importance using the same language.

Role
Business Operations Manager
Focus
Intake · prioritization · governance
Work
Scoring model · role clarity · Jira workflow

The challenge

Requests competed for limited capacity, while value, urgency, and effort could be interpreted differently across teams.

What I designed

  • A common value and urgency scale with clear examples.
  • An effort score that accounts for delivery complexity.
  • A strategic multiplier and a final score to support, not replace, discussion.
  • Role-based checks so requesters, assignees, and leads know what they own.

Why it helps

Teams can compare requests consistently, make trade-offs explicit, and revisit a score when assumptions change.

CASE / 03 KNOWLEDGE ARCHITECTURE

Connect research
to the decisions it informs.

I mapped a lightweight knowledge model that links research and experiment learning, then outlined an AI assistant concept that answers from cited team sources.

Role
Business Operations Manager
Focus
Research discovery · reusable learning
Stage
Investigation · proposed MVP · assistant concept

The challenge

Research notes, hypotheses, tests, and results lived in different places. Teams could not easily trace what evidence informed a test or what the test taught them.

What I designed

  • A connected model for Insight, Hypothesis, Experiment, and Result.
  • A small pilot using a limited set of completed cases before wider migration.
  • A search assistant concept that returns concise answers with links back to source material.
  • Clear separation between structured summaries and full reports.

Project stage

The knowledge hub was an MVP recommendation, not a launched system. The assistant UI demonstrates a concept; the embedded prototype below uses demo data.

into clear product rules.

CASE / 05 TEAM ENABLEMENT

Make career expectations
easier to act on.

I helped organize a level-based capability framework so teammates and leads could discuss role expectations with shared, observable examples.

Role
Growth team enablement
Focus
Product capability · career growth
Work
Level rubric · calibration worksheet

The challenge

Career conversations are difficult when levels are described in broad terms. People need concrete examples of how scope, judgment, and collaboration grow over time.

What I structured

  • Capability areas that connect product understanding, customer context, metrics, and technical collaboration.
  • Observable examples that distinguish junior, middle, and senior scope.
  • A calibration worksheet that makes strengths and development areas easier to discuss.

Sample status
